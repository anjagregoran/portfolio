01

Orbita prijevodi

Translation agency · Redesign

What the client needed

Orbita is a translation agency with offices in Kastav and Zagreb. They wanted a site that looks as professional as the work they deliver and makes it easy for a client to ask for a quote.

What I built

I designed a new site around an interactive globe that travels from language to language. All eight services and more than thirty languages are now easy to browse, and the quote form prepares the enquiry for the client.

Visit the site

02

More Café & Brunch

Café and brunch · Concept site

What the client needed

More is a café and brunch concept planned for two homes, Marcellus in Michigan and Kastav in Croatia. It needed a website that already feels like the place itself, unhurried, warm and welcoming, before the doors open.

What I built

I designed a calm site in cream and sage green, with the whole menu in one place and a separate page that tells the story of the two homes, so a guest knows what to expect before walking in.

Visit the site

03

Abroad Life with Anja

Personal brand · Guides and store

What the site needed

This is my own site for students who dream of a high school exchange year in the USA. It had to bring my guides, my story and my community together in one place, in English and in Croatian.

What I built

I built a bilingual site that leads a student step by step from the first idea to the year itself. The guides arrive by email, and alongside them there is a bundle page, a store page and a page for future host families.

Visit the site
