Questions

Good to know

How long does it take?

We agree on the timing together. A simple site can be ready within a week, as long as I have your texts and photos.

What about my domain?

If you already have one, I connect your new site to it and your email stays exactly as it is. If you do not, I help you choose and register one.

Can my site be in English and Croatian?

Yes. I build sites in one or both languages, which makes a big difference if you have guests or clients from abroad.

What if I want to change something later?

Just send me a message with what you would like changed, and I take care of it as part of your monthly upkeep.

How much does it cost?

Every business needs something a little different, so after a short conversation I send you a clear price with no surprises.
